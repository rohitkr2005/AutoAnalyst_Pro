1. Architectural Commitment

AutoAnalyst Pro ("AutoAnalyst", "we", "us", or "our") provides an autonomous enterprise business intelligence, automated dataset cleansing, relational star-schema synthesis, and DAX query computation platform.

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of technical and operational data when users interact with the AutoAnalyst Pro web platform, API endpoints, and analytical workspace studios. Our technical architecture is intentionally engineered around data minimization and transient memory computation: your analytical data is never commodified, sold, or utilized for foundational machine learning model pre-training.

2. Information We Process

We classify data processed within AutoAnalyst Pro into three strict tiers:

2.1 Account & Identity Data

When you register for an AutoAnalyst workspace, we collect your full name, username, enterprise email address, and an encrypted hash of your password (argon2id / bcrypt standard). If you authenticate through single-sign-on (Google OAuth 2.0), we receive your verified email address and public account identifier from your identity provider.

2.2 Uploaded Dataset Payloads (Customer Data)

Customer Data includes tabular datasets, CSVs, Excel workbooks (XLSX), Parquet files, SQL schemas, and JSON exports that you upload into the AutoAnalyst cleansing and relational modeling engines.

2.3 Technical Telemetry & Operational Metrics

To maintain platform reliability and prevent denial-of-service disruptions, our servers log standard HTTP request metadata, including IP addresses, browser user-agent tokens, endpoint response latency, and system resource utilization. These operational logs are retained solely for diagnostic telemetry and are automatically rotated.

3. Zero Data Retention Architecture

In-Memory Ephemeral Execution

When you ingest a dataset into AutoAnalyst Pro, processing, automated schema resolution, anomaly detection, and DAX calculations occur strictly in ephemeral volatile memory (RAM).

Unlike legacy BI systems that mirror your entire enterprise warehouse into permanent SaaS persistence stores, AutoAnalyst Pro processes customer uploads in isolated worker sandboxes.

  • No Permanent Storage of Raw Data: We do not store raw customer CSV or Excel rows on persistent disks unless you explicitly click "Save to Workspace Vault".
  • Session Eviction: When your authenticated session terminates, or when you trigger "Clear Workspace Data", all in-memory DataFrames and DAX semantic trees are immediately garbage collected and securely purged from memory.
  • No AI Model Training: None of your proprietary financial metrics, healthcare records, operational KPIs, or DAX measure formulations are ever transferred to third-party generative AI vendors or utilized to fine-tune external machine learning models.

4. DAX & Relational Model Security

The AutoAnalyst Relational Modeler synthesizes Star-Schema designs and calculates DAX expressions (including CALCULATE, DIVIDE, SUMX, and Time Intelligence) completely inside your private process boundary.

When you execute the DAX Copilot, only your table schema structure (column names, data types, and high-level cardinality metrics) is examined to recommend measure formulations. No raw row values or customer identifying records are leaked in the synthesis process.

5. Cryptography & Vault Standards

We apply defense-in-depth cryptographic controls across all platform surfaces:

  • Data in Transit: All HTTP traffic is enforced over TLS 1.3 encryption with strict HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS) headers. Direct HTTP calls are automatically rejected.
  • Data at Rest: Account credentials, workspace configurations, and generated executive presentation templates are stored using AES-256 block encryption.
  • Database Isolation: PostgreSQL data layers are governed by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring complete multi-tenant tenant isolation; no user or workspace can query records belonging to another tenant identifier.
  • Session Integrity: Session tokens are signed using cryptographic SHA-256 HMAC tokens with HttpOnly, Secure, and SameSite=Lax security attributes.

6. Cloud Providers & Sub-processors

AutoAnalyst Pro leverages enterprise cloud infrastructure providers adhering to ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and FedRAMP certification standards:

  • Render Services, Inc.: Web application hosting, container orchestration, and server execution environments in high-availability US/EU regions.
  • Supabase Inc. / AWS: Managed cloud database hosting, managed PostgreSQL storage, and enterprise identity infrastructure.
  • Google Cloud Identity: OAuth 2.0 delegated authentication provider for single-sign-on verification.

7. Rights Under GDPR & CCPA

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you maintain comprehensive privacy prerogatives:

  • Right to Access: You may request an exported copy of all account data and workspace metadata associated with your profile.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate profile details at any time from your account settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may permanently purge your user profile, saved workspaces, and relational metadata with zero retention.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may withdraw consent for optional product telemetry at any time.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any user exercising their statutory privacy rights.

8. Cookies & Local Session Storage

AutoAnalyst Pro uses minimal cookies and HTML5 local storage strictly necessary for platform functionality:

  • session: Encrypted authentication session cookie required to maintain your login state.
  • analytica_theme_pref: Client-side local storage key storing your visual interface preference (dark or light).
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site retargeting pixels, or invasive behavioral beacons.

9. Retention & Account Erasure

We retain your account profile details only for the duration of your active subscription. If you choose to close your account, your authentication record and all associated workspace configurations are permanently deleted from our live production databases within 48 hours.
